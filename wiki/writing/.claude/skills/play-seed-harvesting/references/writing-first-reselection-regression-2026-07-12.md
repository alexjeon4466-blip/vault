# Writing-first re-selection regression — 2026-07-12

## Trigger

Use this reference when harvested public-source or book-derived seeds keep converging on props, bodily tasks, blocking exercises, or live/material tests as the immediate next step.

## Failure pattern

A valid test for one geometry-dependent seed was generalized into a universal gate. Two public-source batches and one book-origin batch then favored candidates that were easy to demonstrate with objects. The immediate recommendations asked the writer to leave the computer and stage towels, keys, maps, tools, or floor routes before writing. This caused otherwise interesting seeds to be held and made writing look like an output permitted only after workshop proof.

The deeper error was not merely recommending the wrong probe. Selection itself had been biased by testability:

- clear object/task was mistaken for strong seed;
- missing desire/counterforce/cost was treated as failure rather than a writing question;
- A/B/F, natural scale, and provisional form appeared before user attraction;
- writer curiosity arrived after structural diagnosis;
- voice, relation, silence, memory, point of view, and time gaps were underweighted.

## Corrected class-level sequence

1. **Preserve a broad source pool.** Keep provenance and protected source pressure. Capture people, voices, relations, silence, time, viewpoint, action, image, and objects without requiring all of them.
2. **Re-read doctrine-free.** Ignore prior A/B/F, `유망★`, form, duration, and live-test labels. Ask what person, next line, silence, relation, or time remains unfinished enough to write.
3. **Show 0–3 per batch.** Label them `사용자 선택 대기`, not promoted works. Zero is valid. Do not rank by ease of testing.
4. **Wait for user attraction.** The user selects 0–1. Before that, create no writing-note, B2, draft, form table, or material test.
5. **Match one probe to the uncertainty.**
   - person/voice/relation/silence → 2–5 page scene or monologue;
   - repetition/time jump → scene pair or beat sketch;
   - source/ethics → research or fictionalization boundary;
   - geometry/distance/weight/audience legibility → safe material, blocking, or sound test.
6. **Let text generate workshop questions.** Physical testing is usually downstream of a concrete textual question, not permission to begin writing.
7. **After the probe, choose among:** write further, try another scene, research, merge, hold, or only then compare form/structure.

## Re-selection card

For each user-facing candidate, record only:

- protected source detail;
- why a person/voice/relation/time/question remains writable;
- how it differs from the writer's recurrent machinery;
- one first writing question;
- current state: `사용자 선택 대기 / 병합 재료 / 보류 / 제외`.

A possible probe may be named conditionally, but it must not read as an assigned next task before selection.

## Book-origin application

Bookclub interpretation, questions, and lectures remain independently valuable. Source mining may preserve syntax, silence, relation, viewpoint, memory, temporal gaps, bodies, and objects. Do not require a complete present action or stage route before the writer may try a fragment.

When a past hold was caused by an unwanted physical-test recommendation, preserve the hold. Do not auto-reopen. On explicit request, re-read the whole source-derived pool under the corrected logic rather than reviving the old top three.

## Verification checklist

- User selection occurred before A/B/F, form, duration, B2, and live-test work.
- `사용자 선택 대기` means presentation, not promotion.
- Each candidate has a writer-facing reason independent of prop mechanics.
- No immediate recommendation forces a context switch away from the writer's desk.
- Material testing appears only beside a concrete audience-legibility bottleneck.
- Existing user holds remain holds unless explicitly reopened.
- Source and bookclub assets remain valuable even when selected seeds = 0.
