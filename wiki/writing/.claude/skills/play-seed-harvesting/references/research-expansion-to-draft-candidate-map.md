# Research expansion → form comparison → selected promotion

Use when an initial public-source harvest produces promising seeds and the user asks for deeper research or promotion.

## Boundary

Research can sharpen source pressure, structural DNA, and more than one formalization. It does not default to a one-act, decide B2 inside a seed card, or promote merely because a semi-public procedure is available. Current form/length decisions come from the vault doctrine and `playwriting-dramaturgy`.

## Workflow

1. **Read the seed and source first.** Preserve provenance, access boundaries, protected novelty, and what the source actually makes people/bodies/materials do.
2. **Expand by materially different source lanes.** Search for actions, relations, temporal patterns, sensory facts, objects, sounds, exact language, and conflicting accounts. Official/status language is one possible lane, not the default.
3. **Update structural DNA.** Record place/route, relation organization, counterforce, time fuel, action/material, ethical distance, and missing fuel.
4. **Write a research-development note.** Save under `wiki/writing/notes/<seed>_리서치확장.md` with source tables and a ranking of which cards gained theatrical pressure.
5. **Generate 2–3 formalizations.** Always include the smallest natural scale. Other candidates must differ by action/time organization: continuous stay, vertical repetition, multi-flow, longitudinal consequence, journey/ritual, or discontinuous/cycle inheritance.
6. **Run self-copy check.** Reject invented government desk, missing form field, status screen, kind worker exception, or quiet residue when the source does not supply them.
7. **Invoke canonical selection.** Use `playwriting-dramaturgy` and the vault doctrine to compare protected achievement, new action, time fuel, missing fuel, downstream/explanation/production cost, and full-length/cycle threshold. Obtain user selection unless parallel exploration was explicitly requested.
8. **Promote one current selection or hold.** Use the appropriate map/draft filename; do not force `단막후보_맵`. B2 begins only after selection.
9. **Update wiki cockpit.** Patch index, lineage/status, and log when user-facing; run dashboard tests if affected.

## Research-development output

- source/provenance table;
- source pressure and protected novelty;
- new stageable action/material;
- revised structural DNA;
- missing fuel and ethical distance;
- 2–3 form hypotheses with distinct time fuel;
- full-length/cycle threshold;
- self-copy risk;
- recommendation **and** hold option;
- explicit handoff to canonical form selection.

## Pitfalls

- More research is not more issue summary.
- Exact official phrases are useful only when the source actually contains institutional pressure.
- Do not merge every researched item into one play.
- Do not let a filename or old map schema decide the form.
- Preserve public-only boundaries and fictionalize identifiable private suffering.
